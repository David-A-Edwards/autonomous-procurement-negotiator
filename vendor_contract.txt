MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Movera Inc. ("Client") and WeWork Global Services ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES PROVIDED
Vendor agrees to provide premium office space, janitorial services, and high-speed internet access at the agreed location for a monthly fee of $12,500.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
(a) Fees: Client shall pay all invoices within seven (7) days of receipt.
(b) Late Fees: Any payment not received by the due date shall incur a late fee of 15% of the total outstanding balance, compounded daily.
(c) Price Increases: Vendor reserves the right to increase the Monthly Fee by up to 20% at any time with 14 days' written notice to Client.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
(a) Initial Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall be 24 months.
(b) Auto-Renewal: This Agreement shall AUTOMATICALLY RENEW for successive 24-month periods unless Client provides written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days prior to the end of the current term.
(c) Termination for Convenience: Vendor may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with 24 hours' written notice to Client. Client may NOT terminate this Agreement for convenience.
(d) Penalty for Early Termination: If Client terminates this Agreement for cause, Client must pay a penalty equal to 100% of the remaining fees for the entire Term.

4. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
(a) Unlimited Liability: Client agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold Vendor harmless against any and all claims, including those arising from Vendor's own negligence.
(b) Cap on Vendor Liability: Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed $500.00 USD.

5. DATA PRIVACY
Vendor shall have the right to monitor, inspect, and sell any data transmitted over the provided internet connection for marketing purposes.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.
